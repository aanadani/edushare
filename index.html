🎓 EduShare

The staffroom,
reimagined online.

A platform built for teachers to organise their own resources, share materials with colleagues, and swap ideas in subject-focused discussion forums.

  • 📁 Keep your personal lesson library in one place
  • 🌐 Share & discover materials from teachers worldwide
  • 💬 Ask questions and trade tips in the forums
  • 📊 Track how often your resources get reused
Prototype · Frontend demo · No real data is stored on a server.
